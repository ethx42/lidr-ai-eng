# Kickoff call: volunteer scheduling tool

Grace (Client, Director): We coordinate about 300 volunteers across our food banks, and scheduling happens over email today.
Omar (Agency): What should volunteers be able to do?
Grace: Volunteers should see open shifts and sign up for them from a web page. They need a reminder email the day before a shift.
Omar: And your coordinators?
Grace: Coordinators need to create shifts for each food bank and see who is signed up. At the end of each month we want a report of volunteer hours per person.
Omar: Any logins today?
Grace: No. Volunteers should log in with their email and a one-time code, nothing to remember.
Omar: Mobile?
Grace: It has to work well on phones; most volunteers sign up from their phone.
