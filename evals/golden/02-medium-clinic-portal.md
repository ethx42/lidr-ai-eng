# Patient portal for a physiotherapy network

Sofía (Client, CEO): We have three physiotherapy clinics. We want a web portal where patients log in, see their upcoming appointments, and download invoices as PDF.
Tomás (Agency, Tech Lead): Where do appointments and invoices live today?
Sofía: Everything is in our practice management system, ClinicCloud. It has a REST API; our IT provider says it's documented.
Tomás: Should patients book through the portal?
Sofía: Yes, patients should be able to book a new session with their physiotherapist, and cancel up to 24 hours before.
Nuria (Client, Head Physio): Patients should also receive their home exercise plans in the portal, as videos we upload.
Tomás: Notifications?
Sofía: An email reminder 24 hours before each appointment.
Tomás: Login method?
Sofía: Email and password is fine. It must comply with GDPR because it's health data.
Tomás: Mobile?
Sofía: Responsive web only, no native app for now.
