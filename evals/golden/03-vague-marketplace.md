# Early idea call

Pablo (Founder): So the idea is kind of like an Airbnb, but for things. People have stuff they don't use and others could rent it.
Ana (Agency): What kind of items?
Pablo: Anything really. Tools, cameras, maybe bikes. We'll see what works.
Ana: Web, app?
Pablo: Probably an app, everyone uses apps. But maybe web first, I don't know.
Ana: How would payments work?
Pablo: We'd take a cut, somehow. Insurance could be a thing too.
Ana: Timeline or budget?
Pablo: As soon as possible, and not too expensive. We need something to show investors.
